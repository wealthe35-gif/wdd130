D Rafting Co

At D Rafting Co, our purpose is to connect people with the raw power and beauty of nature through safe, thrilling river adventures. We are committed to fostering teamwork, building personal resilience, and promoting eco-friendly tourism that protects our waterways for generations to come. Whether you are navigating your very first rapid or conquering a wild river run, we ensure every journey builds lasting memories, unforgettable experiences, and a deeper respect for the great outdoors.

History

Founded in 2014 by a group of passionate outdoor guides, D Rafting Co. started with just two rafts and a shared dream of exploring West Africa's wild rivers. Over the past decade, we have expanded our fleet to provide world-class whitewater adventures for thrill-seekers of all skill levels. From serene family floats to heart-pounding class V rapids, our mission has always remained the same: to deliver unforgettable river experiences centered around safety, environmental conservation, and absolute adventure.
